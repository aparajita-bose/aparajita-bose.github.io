Hello! 😊

I recently completed my **Ph.D.** in **Machine Learning and Graph/Data Mining** from the Dept. of Industrial & Management Engineering at [Hanyang University](https://www.hanyang.ac.kr/web/eng), South Korea, in Feb 2026, where I was advised by [Prof. Byunghoon Kim](https://sites.google.com/hanyang.ac.kr/bigdataanalyticslab/people?authuser=0) in the [Big Data Analytics Lab](https://sites.google.com/hanyang.ac.kr/bigdataanalyticslab/home).

My research focuses on **Artificial Intelligence**, **Machine Learning**, and the development of robust AI systems. I am broadly interested in developing models that can discover latent structures, patterns, and actionable insights from complex real-world data. During my doctoral research, I worked extensively on machine learning, probabilistic modeling, and data-driven approaches to address problems such as **community detection**, **market segmentation**, **link prediction**, **recommendation systems**, and **backbone extraction** with real-world noisy industrial financial transactions data represented as large-scale complex networks. 

Beyond my primary research area, I have also conducted research in **Deep Learning**, **Computer Vision**, and **Multimodal AI**, working on problems such as anomaly detection, wafer bin map defect classification, image super-resolution, and 6D pose estimation for industrial robot bin-picking systems. These experiences provided me with strong foundations in statistical learning, optimization, probabilistic modeling, and scalable machine learning and AI systems.

During my doctoral studies, I was fortunate to publish multiple first-author journal and conference papers. I was also fortunate to receive research funding through a [National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF)](https://eng.nrf.re.kr/) grant supported by the [Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT)](https://www.msit.go.kr/eng/index.do), South Korea, based on my **doctoral thesis**. In addition, I was honored to receive the Hanyang Scholarship for Engineering and Science Scholars.

I have hands-on experience developing AI solutions using Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, and modern machine learning and deep learning frameworks. My broader goal is to develop efficient and trustworthy AI technologies that create meaningful real-world impact and improve people's lives. I am particularly interested in Computer Vision, Generative AI, Multimodal AI, Vision-Language Models, and large-scale machine learning systems.

I am currently seeking opportunities as an AI Research Scientist, Applied Scientist, Machine Learning Scientist, Research Engineer, or Postdoctoral Researcher in industry, research laboratories, or academic organizations.

Outside of research, I enjoy 📷 photography, 🏔️ hiking, and ✈️ traveling and exploring new places 😃😇.

Thank you for visiting my page, and I wish you have a wonderful day ahead! ❤️😊
